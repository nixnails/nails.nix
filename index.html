How To Apply

01 — PUSH BACK

Gently push back your cuticles.

02 — BUFF

Lightly buff the natural nail surface.

03 — CLEAN

Remove dust and oil so the nail is clean and dry.

04 — PRESS ON

Apply glue, place the press-on and hold firmly.

05 — DONE

Enjoy your NIX Nails.
