Nail Sizing Guide

R5COIN

Use a real South African R5 coin next to your natural nails. The R5 coin is 26 mm across, giving us a known reference for sizing.

  1. Remove any press-ons, gel or extensions. Use clean, natural nails.
  2. Place your hand flat on a surface in good natural light.
  3. Place an actual R5 coin directly next to your fingers.
  4. Take a clear photo from directly above, keeping your fingers straight.
  5. Take a separate close-up photo of your thumb with the R5 coin next to it.
  6. Repeat for the other hand if you want both hands sized accurately.
  7. Send the photos through Instagram when you book.

Important: The coin must be a real R5 coin and visible in the same photo as your nails. Don't resize or crop the coin out of the photo.
